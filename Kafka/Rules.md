# Kafka Learning Rules

- Teach exactly one topic at a time.
- Explain first in very simple, beginner-friendly language.
- Use examples and small diagrams when helpful.
- Do not overwhelm with multiple unrelated concepts.
- Build practical understanding before advancing.
- Check understanding with a small question or exercise when appropriate.
- `Progress.md` is the authoritative record of the current learning position.
- When a topic is completed, update `Progress.md` and record the next topic before advancing.
- Never silently skip or reorder roadmap topics.
